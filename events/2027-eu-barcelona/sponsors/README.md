# Sponsors

Sponsor and community partner logos and sponsor announcement graphics, in one place. The social media and marketing teams use the graphics here to promote sponsors, and the website uses the logos.

- **Add a sponsor once their contract is signed.**
- **Logos** go in `logos/`, exactly as the sponsor sends them: SVG if they have one, plus PNG. Don't recolour or redraw them (see [sponsors and partners](../../../foundations/brand/#7-sponsors-and-partners) in the brand guidelines). Name like `<company>-logo-color.svg`.
- **Announcement graphics** go in `announcements/`, at the post sizes in the [launch kit](../launch/). Name like `rejekts-<year>-<region>-<city>-sponsor-<company>-1080x1350.png`.

The sponsorship prospectus is designed in Illustrator with the sponsorship team and published in the [Sponsorship repo](https://github.com/RejektsConference/Sponsorship), where future teams can find it.
